What is SUHS?

SUHS provides an alternative namespace and gateway system for participating websites and services. The registry maintains domain ownership, administrative relationships, profiles and virtual mailbox identities.

The Registry

.suhs

The public namespace used for websites, applications, projects and services.

.root

The paired administrative namespace used to manage the corresponding public .suhs domain.

Profile Identity

Every registered owner can have a registry profile associated with their SUHS identity.

Virtual Email

SUHS profiles can receive mail through their allocated SUHS email identity.
